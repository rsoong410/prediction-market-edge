Column guide

r (thresh-matched)
Pearson r built at the EXACT Kalshi line threshold, not the median. Both binary series use the same threshold from Kalshi pricing.
CI lo
Fisher 95% lower bound on r. Positive = correlation distinguishable from 0 at 95% confidence.
Kalshi p_a / p_b
Ask prices from live Kalshi orderbook — the price you actually pay for yes.
Model joint
p_a·p_b + r·√(p_a(1−p_a)·p_b(1−p_b)) — bivariate Bernoulli. Higher than p_a·p_b because r > 0.
Edge ratio
Model joint / (p_a·p_b). Above 1.0 means correlation adds lift over independent price.
Gross edge
Model joint − p_a·p_b in cents per $1 parlay unit = r·√(p_a(1−p_a)·p_b(1−p_b)).
RT cost
(ask_a − bid_a) + (ask_b − bid_b): one-way spread cost to enter both legs.
Net edge
Gross edge − RT cost. Positive = correlation lift exceeds entry spread. This is the gate.
Stability
H1/H2/L20 r values. Flagged if r flips sign between halves or L20 contradicts full-season sign.
