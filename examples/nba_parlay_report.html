Column guide

n
Shared games -- games where both players had resolved Kalshi markets
r
Pearson correlation of surprise series (outcome - implied prob)
CI lo
Fisher 95% CI lower bound on r (conservative floor on true correlation)
Avg Prob
Average Kalshi pregame implied probability per leg
Joint Hit %
Fraction of shared games where ALL legs hit -- observed in-sample
Indep %
Product of Avg Probs -- expected joint hit rate under independence
Edge Ratio
Joint Hit % / Indep % -- above 1.2x clears typical parlay vig
